    About

    Good design starts with a tape measure.

    Space.Design is an independent resource that helps people plan, measure and design the spaces they live and work in.

    Most design mistakes aren't about taste — they're about size, light and flow: a sofa that blocks a doorway, a rug that's too small, a kitchen without enough task lighting. We build free tools and write practical guides that solve those problems before money is spent.

    What we offer

    • Free tools: a 2D room planner and nine calculators for paint, flooring, rugs, lighting, budgets, wallpaper, furniture fit, art and colour.
    • Guides: original, measurement-first articles reviewed and updated regularly.
    • Matching: a free service connecting homeowners with suitable designers and contractors.
    • Community: monthly design contests celebrating real homes.

    Editorial standards

    Our guides are written to be practical and accurate. Figures such as clearances and cost ranges are general guidance — local codes, products and prices vary, and we always recommend confirming with qualified local professionals. Sponsored content is always labelled.

    How we're funded

    Space.Design is supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, partnerships and voluntary donations. Advertisers never influence our editorial recommendations.
