## Features adopted across the benchmark
- **Lead funnel:** category tiles, a 4-step qualification form, a progress bar, trust badges and a promise to reply within one business day. Modelled on Angi, Thumbtack, Houzz and Havenly.
- **Quiz to match:** a style quiz whose result leads to a designer match. Modelled on Havenly and Decorilla.
- **Tools to CTA:** each calculator result links to quotes. Modelled on Behr and HomeAdvisor.
- **Planner:** true-scale drag, rotate and export. Modelled on IKEA, Floorplanner and RoomSketcher.
- **Editorial:** a table of contents, data tables, related articles and Article schema. Modelled on The Spruce and Bob Vila.
- **Freshness:** an automatic news and video feed. Modelled on Dezeen, ArchDaily and Design Milk.
- **Community:** monthly contests with official rules and sponsors. Modelled on Dezeen Awards, designboom and Apartment Therapy.
- **Supply side:** a pro application form and an advertise/media-kit page. Modelled on Houzz Pro and the Dezeen jobs board.
