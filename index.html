    Frequently asked questions

    Is Space.Design really free?

    Yes. All tools, guides and the matching service are free for homeowners and renters. We're supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations.

    How does designer matching work?

    You describe your space, style, budget and location. We review it and introduce you to professionals who fit. You decide whether to work with anyone — there's no obligation.

    Do the calculators work in metric?

    Every calculator has a feet/metres switch.

    Is my room plan saved?

    Your plan is stored only in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you export it.
