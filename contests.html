    Official rules (summary)

    Eligibility

    Open to individuals aged 18+ where permitted by law. Void where prohibited. Employees of Space.Design and contest sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible.

    No purchase necessary

    No purchase or payment is required to enter or win. Donations to Space.Design do not improve your chances.

    Entries & rights

    One entry per person per month. You must own the photos. By entering you grant Space.Design a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your entry with credit on this website and its social channels.

    Winners & prizes

    Winners are selected by the judging criteria above, notified by email within 14 days of the close, and must respond within 14 days or an alternate is chosen. Prizes are non-transferable; taxes are the winner's responsibility. Where required by law, a skill-testing question may apply.

    Privacy

    Entry information is used only to administer the contest, as described in our Privacy Policy.
