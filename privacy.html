    Privacy Policy

    Last updated 2026-11-16

    This policy explains what information Space.Design ("we", "us") collects and how it's used. By using this website you agree to this policy.

    Information you give us

    When you submit a form (quote request, contact, contest entry, application, pledge) we receive the details you enter, such as name, email, phone, location and project information. We use it only to respond to you and provide the service you asked for — for example, introducing you to suitable professionals when you request matching. We do not sell your personal information.

    Forms are delivered to us by email through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit), which processes the submission solely to deliver it.

    Information stored in your browser

    Room plans, theme choice, quiz results and cookie preferences are stored in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.

    Cookies and advertising

    We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

    You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    Embedded content

    Videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play. Embedded content behaves as if you visited the other website and may collect data under that site's policy.

    Your rights

    Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA and India's DPDP Act), you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

    Retention & security

    We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were sent and to meet legal obligations. The site is served over HTTPS.

    Children

    This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

    Changes

    We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
