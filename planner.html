    Free tool

    2D room planner

    Set your room size, add true-to-scale furniture, drag to arrange and press R to rotate or Delete to remove. Your plan saves automatically in this browser.

    Walkways

    Keep 36" (3 ft) for main paths and 30" minimum elsewhere. Each grid square is 1 ft.

    Footprint

    Aim to keep furniture under 50–60% of the floor. The live stat turns orange when a room is crowded.

    Next step

    Happy with the layout? Send it to a designer for finishes, lighting and a shopping list.
